======================================================================
OVERALL PHASE 4 CONCLUSION
======================================================================

Phase 3 established a robust, multiply-validated correlational localization
of the retrieval-override signature (Gemma: layers 27-38; Llama: layers 2-4).
Phase 4 tested whether this localization is causally load-bearing.

Three approaches were tested, in order of increasing methodological rigor:
  1. Attention-head ablation alone (block-level) -- see Section 1 above.
  2. MLP ablation alone (wholesale, then per-layer screen) -- see Section 2.
  3. Joint attention+MLP ablation with a sensitive continuous outcome metric,
     modeled on the approach used successfully in ReDeEP (Sun et al., ICLR 2025)
     -- see Section 3. This is the definitive result; read Section 3's p-values
     to determine which conclusion applies:

  IF SIGNIFICANT (p<0.05, real > control): the identified layers are causally
  validated as driving override behavior, confirming Phase 3's localization is
  not merely correlational.

  IF NOT SIGNIFICANT: across all three tested approaches, the correlational
  signature could not be causally validated via targeted ablation. This is
  consistent with a distributed, redundant implementation of the mechanism
  (supported by Phase 3's own finding that 40-60% of heads in the block carry
  signal) rather than a small set of causally necessary components. This is a
  legitimate, informative negative result, not a failure of the investigation.
